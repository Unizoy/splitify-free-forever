## Summary

- **Who owes whom on the group page:** under the status pill, up to 3 of my lines ("Asha owes you ₹1,200",
  "You owe Neha ₹300"), exactly the Balances tab's rows (simplify followed, main currency first), "+1 more" to the
  Balances tab, a chevron that folds them (kept per group on the phone) and "Not synced yet" for pending rows.
- **Settled history:** once everyone is square in every currency, everything up to the last settle-up folds into
  "Show N settled expenses" at the end of the Expenses tab.
- **Groups filter:** a small pill next to "Active groups · N": All / Outstanding / I owe / Owe me, kept on the
  phone, with "No groups match · Show all".
- **Trip dates and settle-up reminders:** trips get Starts / Ends rows (calendar sheet, no end = no end, no dialog
  on save); every group gets Off · Weekly · Monthly with "Next reminder: …". The group page shows "12–18 Dec".
  The server nudges members with a balance at 10:00 IST: weekly on the creation weekday, monthly on the 1st, and
  the day after a trip ends — once per group per day, through activity → notifications → push (muted = in-app).
- **Group notes (whiteboard):** one text page per group (`/groups/:id/notes`), "Last edited by Asha · 21 Nov",
  offline through the outbox, conflicts keep both versions, in-app notification only. Settings row + "Notes" chip.
- **Search from the group page:** a search icon in the hero opens search with the group picked.
- **Activity:** nudges (bell icon, Payments filter, open Balances), notes edits (open the notes), reminder settings;
  a deleted expense / payment opens its page (Deleted + Restore).
- **Backup:** the JSON's `group` carries the trip dates, reminder setting and notes (still version 1).
