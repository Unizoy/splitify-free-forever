## Summary

- **Settle everything with one friend (B1):** a friend's Settle up sheet starts with "Everything with Asha · owes you
  ₹455 · 2 groups" (per currency). It opens the same one-screen Record a payment, prefilled with the total, with
  "Across 2 groups ›" for the breakdown. Saving writes one payment per group through the outbox; part of it pays the
  biggest group balance first; more than the total is an inline error; one Pay-via-UPI link carries the total.
- **Restore from the payment page (B2):** a deleted payment opened from Activity shows "Deleted" and Restore.
- **Share a reminder (B3):** people without the app get a share button that opens a ready-made message (with my
  UPI link for rupees) for the Android share sheet; a push that cannot go offers "Share instead".
- **Personal friend code (B4, migration 0025):** Add friend → "Show my code" (QR, code, link, Share, Reset) and "Scan
  a friend's code" (`/friends/code`, also the deep link `splitify://friend?code=…`); both accounts end up linked in
  one friend group.
- **Settled history (B5):** once settled in every currency, the friend page hides the old items behind "Show
  settled items (N)".
