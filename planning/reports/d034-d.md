## Summary

- **Download my data:** Account → Your data makes a `.zip` on the phone, so it works offline. It holds a JSON
  backup of every group (amounts in integer minor units with their currency, unsynced rows marked
  `"pending": true`) and one CSV per group. It goes to the system "Save to" dialog or the share sheet. The
  format is documented in `docs/backup-format.md`.
- **Search:** it also finds payments (by amount, either person, note, method, or "paid") and comments (by
  text):
  - type chips with counts
  - the last 5 searches, kept on the phone
  - opened from a group, it says "Search in Goa trip"
- **Insights:** two new cards. "Category over time" shows 12 monthly bars per category. "Who paid" shows what
  each person paid against their share, month by month, in a group.
- **Totals tab:** "Month" with ‹ › to move through the months, or "All time".
